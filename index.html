Our Story

Named for the heart of the kitchen

In Arabic and Urdu, qalb means heart. It is where good cooking starts — with care, patience and the right ingredients. We began Qalb Spices to bring that care to everyday cooking: whole spices, chosen well, packed fresh and sent to your door.

From the first pinch of pepper in a tadka to a slow-cooked masala that fills the house, spices are what turn a meal into a memory. We want yours to carry a little heart.

Good to Know

Questions, answered

How do I use whole turmeric?

Dried turmeric fingers are hard, so crush them in a mortar or grind them in a strong spice grinder. Start with a little — freshly ground turmeric is bright and potent.

How should I store dry red chillies?

Keep them in an airtight container in a cool, dry, dark place. Kept dry, whole chillies stay good for a long time.

How do I keep whole spices fresh?

Store them airtight, away from heat, light and moisture, and grind only what you need so the aroma stays at its best.
